Receipt recovery
- A later receipt hint received while an older observation is pending now queues
  one further exact read instead of being discarded. Concurrent hints coalesce.
- Selecting the retained command, reconnecting or returning to the foreground
  can observe its receipt again. Observation never dispatches or rebuilds intent.
- The original command ID, payload, settings, session and phase remain frozen.
  Only an authenticated matching 202 consumed receipt clears accepted delivery.
- Local draft ownership includes an opaque edited revision token. It is retained
  beside the command and never enters server/provider input.
- Accepted revision proof is journaled before the final durable outbox entry is
  removed. A document reload before React's cleanup cannot lose this proof.
- A matching accepted revision can clear the submitted composer after reload.
  New text, same-valued newer edits, changed rich draft content/settings and
  another draft owner remain intact. Entries lacking revision proof retain text
  conservatively. They can still recover the command's accepted delivery state.
- Warm acceptance uses the same revision guard as reload recovery. Local
  attachment/context edits retain receipt ancestry only across locally owned
  transitions. A foreign same-valued edit cannot regain older cleanup authority.
- Failed revision replacement first revokes older authority. If both revocation
  operations fail, ordinary and rich draft persistence stay fenced until retry
  succeeds; unsaved edits remain in memory. An unmounted observer leaves cleanup
  proof for the active replacement composer.
- Ambiguous local delivery says 'Delivery not confirmed.' in the existing quiet
  status row. No layout, palette, font or approved settings redesign is introduced.
